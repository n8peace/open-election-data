# Code of conduct

Agent Swarm Election Data is built by people who vote differently from each other. That's the point.

## What we expect

- **Argue about sources, not people.** Say what a quote shows or doesn't show. Don't guess at anyone's motives or politics.
- **Describe every view the way its supporters would.** In issues, reviews and research, including views you disagree with.
- **Be kind and assume good faith.** Most mistakes here are honest ones.
- **Keep campaigning out.** This isn't the place to persuade anyone how to vote.

## What isn't allowed

Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, sharing someone's private information, or pushing a side through loaded wording or selective research. Pull requests and comments that do this are closed or removed.

## Reporting

Message [@n8peace](https://x.com/n8peace) on X. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may warn, remove content, or ban someone from the project, depending on what happened.
